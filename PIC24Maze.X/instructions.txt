=== MAZE QUEST – QUICK REFERENCE MANUAL ===

[ POWER & STARTUP ]
• Turn on the console.
• Choose a difficulty mode.
• Select your preferred maze design.

[ MENU USE ]
• Rotate the POTENTIOMETER → move through menu items.
• Press S1 or S2 → confirm selection.

[ DIFFICULTY MODES ]
• Level 1 → 40s, slow speed.
• Level 2 → 30s, medium speed.
• Level 3 → 20s, fast speed.
• Select with POTENTIOMETER + confirm with S1/S2.

[ MAZE OPTIONS ]
• Maze 1 → Simple layout.
• Maze 2 → Moderate challenge.
• Maze 3 → Complex walls.
• Maze 4 → Extreme pattern.
• Confirm with S1/S2.

[ CONTROL SUMMARY ]
• Tilt board → move ball (accelerometer).
• POTENTIOMETER → vertical scroll.
• Start point: top-right (blue ball).
• Finish: bottom-left (red circle).
• Avoid white wall segments.

[ OBJECTIVE ]
1. Navigate from start to finish.
2. Avoid wall collisions.
3. Beat the countdown timer.

[ WIN CONDITION ]
• Reach finish before timer = victory.
• Higher difficulty = bigger score bonus.

[ LOSS CONDITION ]
• Timer hits zero before finish → game over.

[ TIMER INFO ]
• Time displayed in top-left corner.
• Blinking display under 5 seconds remaining.

[ NAME & SCORE ENTRY ]
• Use POTENTIOMETER to pick letters A–Z.
• S1 → move to next character.
• S2 → save name + score.

[ SCORING FORMULA ]
• Score = Remaining Time × Difficulty × 10.
• Leaderboard shows top 3 scores.

[ EMERGENCY EXIT ]
• Hold S1 or S2 for 3s → quit instantly.
• Score not saved.

=== END OF MANUAL ===
